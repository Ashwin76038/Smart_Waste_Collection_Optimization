## Important Decisions
The operational model is a **simulation demonstration**: synthetic bins/collections, 2026 hypothetical demand, fixed seed, explicitly separate latent truth and observed-only fact. Real OSM coordinates support hypothetical placement but do not make bins real. Missing telemetry remains scheduled rows with null measures. The 2011 Census native geography is not joined to current GCC wards. `data_origin` distinguishes origin; route distance and diesel are assumed proxies, not actual routing. No future weather realization is used to calibrate 2026 demand. See `config/simulation_assumptions.md` and `docs/etl_phase2.md`.

Phase 3 uses latent truth only for retrospective overflow/pre-service-fill diagnostics, not as a deployable prediction feature. Generated mass uses the assumed 0.12 kg/L density. Early (<35% pre-service fill) and late risk (≥95% or positive overflow) are exploratory flags. Zone generation is normalized per hypothetical bin-day. Weekend tests are conditional mechanism checks of a known 1.12 simulator input. Commercial/residential, population-density, policy-effect and route-savings tests were excluded because evidence or comparison scenarios are absent.

## Known Limitations
The simulation produced **501,938 positive-overflow bin-slots (11.46%)**, a warning that arrival/service assumptions need calibration and sensitivity testing. This is not observed Chennai overflow. The 2025 NASA grid and 2026 hypothetical operations have different periods. The current ward-layer effective date is not verified; Census areas are from 2011. OSM POIs/road access are incomplete for truck routing. Route/vehicle daily distances and fuel are formula proxies, so no operational savings, inferential conclusion or forecast accuracy can be claimed. Scanned PDFs need page-level extraction and review before use. The acquired Census India files required a logged TLS certificate exception; it does not resolve license/content validation. The benchmark is one local January measurement and the Excel workbook is only a 500-row QA sample.

Phase 3 generated the equivalent of 18,230.3 tonnes under its fixed density assumption, not measured municipal waste. The latent median pre-service fill is 95.4%, indicating the simulator may be too stressed. The weekly generated-mass contrast is +5,776.9 kg/day (HAC 95% CI 5,428.3–6,125.5) and overflow-slot contrast +1.450 percentage points (CI 1.355–1.544), but these magnitudes and p-values primarily reflect encoded demand/collection rules. One seed/year cannot support external statistical inference. Bin-level correlations partly share construction parameters. The route-distance scatter is a deterministic formula, not efficiency evidence.
